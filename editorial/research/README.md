# Art-reception research cases

`art-reception-cases.json` is a working research dataset of documented responses to early avant-garde works and some later comparators, kept for thinking about whether the historical avant-garde and its opponents treated art's forms, boundaries and cultural authority as more broadly consequential than later audiences have. It is not a representative sample, and the text does not load it.

Each record separates the documented reaction from a possible interpretation, a qualification, and source links. Before a record becomes spine prose, a fund entry or an authored detour, its sources need to be checked and its role in the text decided.
