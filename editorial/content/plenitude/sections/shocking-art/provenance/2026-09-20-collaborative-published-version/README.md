# Collaborative reading of the published version

This directory preserves the first synthetic reading of the published reader-shaped
child `shocking-art-20260920173549-0f38002e`. It used the collaborative OpenAI reader
against the Anthropic guide through the ordinary `evolving` path, not either local
`editorial-*` experiment.

The guide used `shared-evaluative-field` on turn 1 and
`cage-laughter-domestication` on turn 2. The corrected presentation ledger then
withheld both entries for the remaining turns. Section 5 completed during turn 12
and the run advanced to Section 6, rather than becoming trapped in the earlier
skeptical exchange's repeated objection.

The run nevertheless revealed a continuing editorial tendency: after accepting a
useful bounded distinction, the guide repeatedly expanded it into a broad historical
claim about art, institutions, or public response. The collaborative reader then had
to narrow that claim again. The published fund helped articulate two mechanisms, but
did not by itself cure this guide-level tendency.

`session.json` is the machine-readable record. `transcript.md` is the readable
dialogue and includes private reader reflections and the per-turn package/fund-use
record.
