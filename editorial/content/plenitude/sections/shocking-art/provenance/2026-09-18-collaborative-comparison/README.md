# Collaborative spine/fund comparison — 2026-09-18

This directory preserves the first completed matched reading pair for the local
spine-and-fund path. Both sessions used:

- Plenitude, Section 5;
- the collaborative reader profile;
- `gpt-5.6-terra` as reader and the configured Claude guide;
- a maximum of 15 turns; and
- the required structured guide-delivery tool for per-turn fund-use reporting.

Files:

- `spine-session.json` and `spine-transcript.md`: `editorial-spine`, completed after
  10 turns when the reader reached the packaged-section boundary and chose to finish.
- `fund-session.json` and `fund-transcript.md`: `editorial-fund`, reached the 15-turn
  limit after the packaged-section boundary was reached.

The fund guide reported using `shared-evaluative-field` on turn 1 and
`armory-show-ridicule` on turn 2. It did not report using `futurism-and-dada` or
`reach-not-passion`. All guide turns in the final pair used the required structured
tracking tool.

Interpretation must remain cautious. The reader runs are stochastic and were not
given identical questions. The fund session sustained a longer, intellectually useful
exchange, but it also spent many turns repeatedly narrowing the same historical
contrast. That is evidence for the project's central concern: optional material can
help produce conceptual precision while still threatening momentum. It is not yet
evidence that the fund condition is generally better or that either used entry should
be accepted.

An earlier pair from the same day is intentionally not copied here. It exposed two
implementation problems: experimental reading could advance into an unpackaged next
section, and optional-entry tracking through a free-text marker was frequently missing
or incomplete. The section boundary was then enforced in the harness, and tracking was
replaced by a required structured tool before this preserved pair was run.
