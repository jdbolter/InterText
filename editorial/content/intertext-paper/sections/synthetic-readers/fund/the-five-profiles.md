The curious reader is a nonspecialist who follows closely and asks for clarification. The skeptical reader is an academic in a neighbouring field who notices unsupported claims and asks for evidence and counterexamples. The collaborative reader wants the work to be stronger and attends to its prose and movement. The general reader is the educated reader for whom the work is written, who reads for the argument. The student has the reading assigned and asks what terms mean, restating the argument to check it. Some profiles may search the web, but only occasionally and always naming what they found.
