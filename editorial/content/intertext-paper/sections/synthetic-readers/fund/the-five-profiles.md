The curious reader is a nonspecialist who follows closely and asks for clarification. The skeptical reader is an
academic in a neighbouring field who notices unsupported claims and asks for evidence and counterexamples. The
collaborative reader wants the work to be stronger and attends to prose and movement. The general reader is the
educated reader the work is written for, who reads for the argument and writes a few plain sentences. The student is an
undergraduate with the reading assigned, who asks what terms mean and says the argument back to check it. Some may
search the web, at most twice a turn, and must name what they found.
