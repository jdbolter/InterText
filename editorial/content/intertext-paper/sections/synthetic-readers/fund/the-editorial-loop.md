The readings are an editorial instrument, not only a software test. Comparable sessions are run on a known edition and
section; the transcripts and private reflections are read across profiles; an editorial hypothesis is formed rather
than treating any one reply as a verdict; the author's text, the section boundaries, the boxes or the instructions are
revised as the evidence warrants; and the same conditions are run again. Synthetic runs never change the published
text: a preview of how the editor would evolve a section from a reading is never published.
