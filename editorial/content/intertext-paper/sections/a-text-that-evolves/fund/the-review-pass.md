A separate call reviews every proposed change for fidelity, relevance, duplication, prose and rhythm, proportion, the
need for sources, and whether an entry is anchored at a point in the section where what it presupposes has already been
introduced. It may accept an addition, hold it as a candidate that readers do not see, or reject it, and it may not
accept material whose facts still need verification. Structural checks then reject invented source links, unstable
passage identifiers, excessive growth and invalid anchors. If nothing survives, the section is unchanged.
