A separate pass then reviews each proposed change for its fidelity to the work, its relevance, duplication, the quality of its prose and rhythm, its proportion, and its need for sources, and checks that a unit is attached at a point where what it presupposes has already been introduced. The review may accept an addition, hold it as a candidate that readers do not see, or reject it, and it may not accept material whose facts still need to be verified. A further set of checks rejects invented sources and excessive growth. If nothing survives, the section remains as it was.
