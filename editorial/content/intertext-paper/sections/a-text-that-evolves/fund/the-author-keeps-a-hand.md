Routine editorial decisions are left to the model, but the rules are the author's. The author writes or approves the statements of each section's argument, the opening vignettes and the lists of references and media, and sets the policy that the editorial passes follow. A separate editor allows the author to read every section, revise the prose of the spine and any unit of the fund, add units, change their status, and publish the result as a new version. Every unit keeps a record of its origin, so the mixture of hands in an evolved text is preserved.
