The second call receives the draft together with the argument in brief, everything the reader has been shown so far,
the section's text and the reader's words. It returns the reply revised so that it answers the question in its opening
paragraph, introduces anything the reader has not yet met, names people already met by surname alone, keeps each claim
at the strength its evidence allows, cuts praise of the reader and repetition of earlier replies, and stays within 250
words. It may add no ideas and must keep every citation and media marker in place; if it changes one, the draft is used
instead.
