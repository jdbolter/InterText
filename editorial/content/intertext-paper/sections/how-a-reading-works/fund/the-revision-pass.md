The second pass receives the draft together with the statement of the argument, everything the reader has seen so far, the author's text for the section and the reader's words. It revises the reply so that it answers the question in its opening paragraph, introduces anything the reader has not yet met, keeps each claim at the strength its evidence allows, removes praise of the reader and repetition of earlier replies, and stays within 250 words. It may add no new ideas, and every citation must survive it unchanged; otherwise the draft is used instead.
