Besides the spine, each section and each chapter carries a short statement of its argument, written or approved by the author and never shown to the reader. The full text supplies the material; the statement supplies the direction. A section gives a passing example and its central claim roughly equal weight, and a model answering a question about a detail tends to follow the detail. The statement tells the model what is central. It guides every reply, and it is stated only when the reader's question bears on it, in fresh words and as a claim.
