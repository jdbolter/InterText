A section is not a single text that is rewritten and enlarged with each reading. Additions written by a model can be intelligent and supportive and still damage the balance and rhythm of a continuous essay, because a model tends to apply an argument to more cases and to add more qualifications, whether or not they belong in the main movement. The system therefore keeps the concise route through the section, the spine, apart from optional material, the fund, which the text may draw on for a particular reader at a particular moment.
