A section is not one string that is rewritten and enlarged. Additions written by a model can be intelligent and
supportive and still damage the balance and rhythm of a continuous essay, because a model tends to apply an argument
to more cases and add more qualifications whether or not they belong in the main movement. So the concise route
through the section, the spine, is kept apart from optional material, the fund, which the text may select for a
particular reader at a particular moment.
