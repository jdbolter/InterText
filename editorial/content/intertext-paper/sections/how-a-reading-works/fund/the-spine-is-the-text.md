The spine is the author's own text for each section, divided into passages. It is the authoritative route through the section, but the reader never sees it as such. When the reader presses Return, the system retells the next passages in order, closely and often in the author's own phrasing, though compressed and joined to what the conversation has already covered. When the reader asks a question, the system draws on whatever in the spine bears on it and answers in new words.
