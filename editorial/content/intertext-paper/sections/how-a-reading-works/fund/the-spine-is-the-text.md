The spine is the author's own text for the section, divided into passages, each with a stable identifier. It is the
authoritative route through the section, but the reader never sees it as such. When the reader presses Return, the
text retells the next passages in order, closely and often in the author's phrasing, compressed and joined to what
the conversation has already said. When the reader asks a question, the text draws on whatever in the spine bears on
it and answers in new words.
