Each section opens with a short boxed passage written for the reader, which sets a scene and states the section's thesis in brief. The reader may then type a question or comment, which receives an answer, or simply press Return, which carries the reading forward with the next ideas the text holds. The first Return treats the box as a prologue and begins with the first passage; later ones resume where the conversation has reached. When the section's material is exhausted, the text says so, and a further Return moves on to the next section.
