A reply may show one item from the section's checked list of videos, images and web pages, by writing a private token
on a line of its own. The server keeps only listed items, one per reply. The page plays a video only when its
embedding has been checked, shows an image only when its licence has been cleared, with its credit beneath, and shows
anything else as a card that opens in a new tab. Each item on the list says when it is worth showing, and the list is
kept by a script that checks every item rather than from memory.
