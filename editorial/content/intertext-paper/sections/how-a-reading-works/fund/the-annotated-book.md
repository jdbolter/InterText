The reader's page takes the form of an annotated book. The reading is set as a printed page in a Garamond typeface,
with red small capitals for labels and a drop cap opening each section. The reader's own words stand in the margin
beside the reply they drew, in italic, tied to it by a leader line, much as a reader's notes stand beside a printed
text; on narrow screens they stand above the reply instead. The contents and the input sit on a dark column to the
right.
