The reader's page is designed as an annotated book. The reading is set as a printed page in a Garamond typeface, with labels in red small capitals and a drop cap at the start of each section. The reader's own words appear in the margin beside the reply they prompted, in italic and joined to it by a fine line, much as a reader's notes stand beside a printed text. On narrow screens they appear above the reply instead. The contents and the space for typing sit in a dark column on the right.
