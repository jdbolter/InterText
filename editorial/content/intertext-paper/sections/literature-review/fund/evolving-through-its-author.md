The Living Book of AI also changes, but through its author. Francesco Masera chooses a subject each week and passes it
through a series of AI-assisted desks for drafting, editing, fact-checking and images; the Italian edition is updated
continuously and the English one once a year, and at each new edition he decides which updates enter print. Readers'
questions do not alter it. The difference from InterText lies in who moves the text: there the author, on a schedule;
here the readers, in conversation, under rules the author sets.
