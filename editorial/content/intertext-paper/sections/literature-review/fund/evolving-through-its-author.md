The Living Book of AI also changes over time, but the changes come from its author. Francesco Masera chooses a subject each week and passes it through a sequence of AI-assisted editorial desks for drafting, editing, fact-checking and images. The Italian edition is updated continuously and the English edition once a year, and Masera decides at each new edition which updates enter print. Readers' questions do not alter the text. The contrast with InterText lies in who moves the text: there the author, on a schedule; here the readers, in conversation, within rules that the author sets.
