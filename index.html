In 2013 JAY-Z released “Picasso Baby,” a song and a video: the rapper wants to own a Picasso and ends by claiming to be a new Picasso. He is performing in New York’s Pace Gallery, one of the city’s prestigious art spaces. Art-world figures and fans take turns standing across from him while he raps. Marina Abramović, whose MoMA performance of 2010 he was borrowing (she sat motionless for more than two months, staring at one stranger after another), appears as one of his partners. Everyone in the film is delighted that the art world has accepted the rapper as an artist.
