## Further Development

The next step will be to test InterText with human readers. It will admittedly be an uphill battle to convince human readers to change their reading practice significantly. The success of the e-reader, for example, was precisely that it did not require significant change to the linear experience of reading (Baron 2015).

Anticipated further developments include:

- Improved prompts to guide the flow of the argument;
- Making the system more inclusive, e.g. by allowing readers to contribute by name and send comments to the author or other readers.
