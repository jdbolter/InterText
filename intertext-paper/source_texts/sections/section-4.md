## A Text That Evolves

Intertext thus offers two features that distinguish it from most existing systems:

- With the exception of opening section vignettes, there is no canonical text. Instead, the text takes shape in interaction with the reader.
- With the reader’s permission, the system evaluates and absorbs their material into the spine or fund.

The text thus evolves with each reader and each reading. The goal is to facilitate a collaborative text that nevertheless maintains a degree of authorial guidance and control (Compare the interactive narrative authoring system Drama Llama, Sun et al. 2025)
