Before you begin

What you are about to read has already been shaped, in part, by the readers who came before you. When a conversation opens up the argument, takes it somewhere the text hadn't gone, or surfaces a connection it hadn't made, that exchange can be folded back into the essay itself. You can choose to be part of that. If you agree, your conversation today will be used to enrich the sections you engage with — anonymously, as one contribution among many to a text that continues to evolve with its readers.

If you'd rather not contribute, you can still read freely — either the text as it stands today, or the version as it was first written. Either way, nothing about your visit will change what the next reader finds.

Read by pressing Enter, which carries the text on; type at any time to ask, object or add to what it says.
