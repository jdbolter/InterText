[goldberg-2001] Goldberg, Performance Art (2001). From the work's own use (cite only for this point): in Schlemmer's Triadic Ballet (1922) dancers dressed and moved like marionettes in highly synchronized geometric pieces (pp. 111–113). Use: the avant-garde's fascination with the mechanical.

[bogost-2007] Bogost, Persuasive Games (2007). Procedurality is the computer's principal value, creating meaning through the interaction of algorithms (p. 4). Use: why the computer's operation is better called procedural than mechanical.

[murray-1997] Murray, Hamlet on the Holodeck (1997). From the author's notes: the digital environment has four essential properties, procedural, participatory, spatial and encyclopedic (p. 67). Use: procedurality as the computer's defining quality, as digital writers see it.

[carr-2010] Carr, The Shallows (2010). From the work's own use: Carr equated Google's project with Frederick Winslow Taylor's: "By breaking down every job into a sequence of small, discrete steps and then testing different ways of performing each one, Taylor created a set of precise instructions—an 'algorithm.'" Use: a critic who sees the digital as mechanization, missing the changed relation of user and procedure; he argues from the opposite side.
