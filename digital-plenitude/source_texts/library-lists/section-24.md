[csikszentmihalyi-1991] Csikszentmihalyi, Flow (1991). From a summary and the work's own use: flow is "a state in which people are so involved in an activity that nothing else seems to matter; the experience itself is so enjoyable that people will do it even at great cost, for the sheer sake of doing it" (p. 4); it requires concentration, and playing the piano is a stronger flow experience than listening to it (pp. 108–113). Use: flow defined, and the point at which the media flow of games and social media goes beyond his definition.

[watercutter-2014] Watercutter, "Edge of Tomorrow Is the Best Videogame You Can't Play" (2014). From the work's own use: the Wired review calls the film "the best videogame you can't play" and says of its romance that it is "probably necessary just because, you know, people like having feelings at movies, but still feels tacked on." Use: a gamer's view of a cathartic film.

[morris-2016] Morris, "Level Up! Video Game Industry Revenues Soar in 2015" (2016). From the work's own use, as corrected (cite only for this point): revenues for video game sales in the United States totaled $23.5 billion in 2015. Use: the economic importance of games.
