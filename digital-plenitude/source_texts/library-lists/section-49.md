[bourdieu-1984] Bourdieu, Distinction (1984). From the publisher's description and the work's own use (cite only for these points): judgements of taste are socially structured; the dominant cultural and economic classes agreed on standards, and the tastes of other classes were largely ignored. Use: why there was never a golden age of shared standards, only the agreement of the dominant classes.

[mcluhan-1964] McLuhan, Understanding Media (1964). Each medium, by its technical form rather than its content, extends the senses and changes human nature and culture; "the medium is the message" is the title of the first chapter. Use: the popular modernist forecasts, in McLuhan's tradition, that one technology will remake our culture.

[burger-1984] Bürger, Theory of the Avant-Garde (1984). After the historical avant-garde, the radically disparate exists side by side, and no movement can claim to be historically more advanced than any other (p. 63). Use: multiplicity as our future, stated decades earlier for art.

[csikszentmihalyi-1991] Csikszentmihalyi, Flow (1991). Flow is a state of involvement so complete that nothing else seems to matter (p. 4). Use: one pole of the first dichotomy, flourishing beside catharsis.
