[schrank-2014] Schrank, Avant-Garde Videogames (2014). From the abstract and the work's own use: an avant-garde game opens the closed circuit of play and draws attention to itself as a game; JODI's Untitled Game modifies the Quake engine (p. 53); Space Giraffe overwhelms the player with "fire flowers and 'sparkling grunts'" (p. 50); Photopia presents a disrupted story (p. 179); in Passage "the player can walk any path … but she will always die in five minutes, in a gentle, morose irony" (p. 193). Use: games that refuse flow, and reflection as their aim.

[flanagan-2009] Flanagan, Critical Play (2009). From the preface and contents (cite only for this point): play can be a mode of critique, and games can be designed for it. Use: critical play beside Schrank's avant-garde games.

[wardrip-fruin-2020] Wardrip-Fruin, How Pac-Man Eats (2020). From the publisher's description (cite only for these points): games mean through operational logics and playable models, and designers widen what games can express by stretching a logic, as indie, art and research games do. Use: how art games bend the conventions of mainstream play.

[reese-2016] Reese, "An Exhibition That Proves Video Games Can Be Art" (2016). From the work's own use (cite only for this point): Jason Rohrer became the first video game creator to have a solo show at a significant museum. Use: art games and the art world.

[greenberg-1960] Greenberg, "Modernist Painting" (1960). From a teaching summary and the work's own use: modernism is self-criticism, each art establishing what is unique to its medium, and modernist art "call[s] attention to art." Use: reflection as the aim of elite modern art.

[greenberg-1939] Greenberg, "Avant-Garde and Kitsch" (1939). From the work's own use (cite only for this point): a work was either avant-garde or kitsch, serious and difficult for an elite, or easy and sentimental for the masses. Use: the opposition that today's popular reflection dissolves.

[lessig-2008] Lessig, Remix (2008). Girl Talk's Night Ripper is built from some 200 to 250 samples (pp. 11–15). Use: hip-hop and mashup remix as popular reflection that also flows, which chapter 6 takes up.
